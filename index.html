    Why it matters for Sublime Comporta

    Concept prototype, not a live product. Availability, times and guest details are illustrative. Prices (spa, Chef in Villa, Barman in Villa, wine tastings, romantic dinner, the Yoga, Tea & Fado Retreat), restaurant hours and seasons, rooms and policies are taken from the public Sublime Comporta website and its PDFs; time slots for the spa, padel and yoga, padel pricing and the retreat's daily schedule are placeholders (the site does not publish them). In production this connects to the resort's PMS (HOST) for reservations, room charges and guest data, plus the dining and spa booking systems: the integration is the first thing to scope.
